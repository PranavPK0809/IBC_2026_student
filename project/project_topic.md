\# Final Project Topic



For my final project, I was thinking about using an NHANES health dataset in CSV format. The dataset includes diabetes status along with different physiological and lifestyle measurements.



Some of the questions I am interested in looking at are:



\- How does diabetes prevalence vary across different BMI categories?

\- How do physiological measurements such as BMI, waist circumference, cholesterol, and blood pressure differ between individuals with and without diabetes?

\- Do lifestyle factors such as physical activity and sleep differ between individuals with and without diabetes?



I am still deciding which questions and variables would be best to focus on for the final project, so I would appreciate any feedback on the direction.
